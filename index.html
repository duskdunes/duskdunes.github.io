Dusk Dunes

Click to look around. W A S D or arrow keys to walk. Hold Ctrl (or double-tap W) to dash. Shift goes down when flying, swimming or diving. In Zen Wander, double-tap Space to fly. Esc to pause.

On iPhone: you always move forward. Tilt the phone or swipe to steer. Touch and hold to brake, tap to jump. Flick the phone up to jump, down to dive.

Full screen: in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open it from your home screen to hide the browser bars.

Effects (stack with any view, and each other)

Multiplayer: host a game and share the 4-letter code.

Settings

Keys: M changes the 3D view, [ and ] change depth.

Accessories and pets

Cosmetic only. Other players see your look.

CPUs

CPUs join as extra players with their own names and skill level. In multiplayer, the host decides.

Account (optional)

Sign up to keep your settings, look, pet and CPUs. You can play without one.

Signed in as . Changes are saved automatically.

Your password is never stored or sent. It is turned into a login value and an encryption key in your browser, and your settings are encrypted with that key. There is no password recovery.

Sync across devices

Accounts live on this device unless you connect a sync server (see sync/README.md in the source). With one, sign up once and log in anywhere.

Cheats can be switched off for everyone: press X five times quickly.
